## Improved

- **Mods → Profile** in the menu bar switches profiles (the one in use is checked), opens a profile's collection, and manages profiles.
- The Dock icon shows how many mod updates are waiting.
- The menu bar icon's menu shows the profile in use on its first line.
- **View → Show Details** (⌥⌘I) works on the page you're on: on the SMAPI Log, it shows or hides the loaded mods.
- Updates says "All up to date" when there's nothing to install.
- Saves: the install button reads "Install Selected" until you tick a mod.

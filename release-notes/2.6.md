## New

- **Menu bar icon, on by default.** Play, switch profiles and see mod updates without opening the window. Its Profile menu now also has Manage Profiles… and, for a profile made from a collection, Show Collection…. You can turn the icon off in Settings → General.

## Improved

- The profile in use is checked in every profile menu (menu bar, Installed, SMAPI Log, Saves).
- Collection pictures are shown whole in the Collections list and the collection window, instead of cropped to a square.

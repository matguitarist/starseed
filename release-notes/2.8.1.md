## Steadier downloads and installs

- A mod or SMAPI download that loses its connection, even for a Wi-Fi drop, picks up where it stopped, and a cut download never reaches the installer.
- A mod archive that would write outside its folder is refused, and an install or restore that fails halfway leaves your Mods and saves as they were.
- Installing mods, backing up saves and updating SMAPI can no longer hang on a tool with a lot to say.
- The Storage page counts hard-linked files once, so the sizes match what the disk really holds.
- A refused download now says the server's real error code.

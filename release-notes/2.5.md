## New

- **Light or dark.** Settings → General → Theme: follow macOS, or always light or always dark.
- **Settings, reorganized.** General, Game, Updates, Nexus Mods and Storage tabs. The Game tab gathers your game, SMAPI, the Mods folder and the options around playing; Updates has Check Now for mods and for Starseed. Settings reopens on the tab you used last.

## Improved

- Mod and collection pictures keep their shape in the details panel: portrait pictures are shown whole instead of cropped.
- The Nexus Mods settings show your account, with Change Key… and Remove, instead of an empty key field.
- Console Commands and Save Backup, the two mods that come with SMAPI, explain that they're optional.
- Find a Broken Mod keeps SMAPI's own mods on and leaves them out of the search, so it needs one test fewer.

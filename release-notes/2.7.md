## A better SMAPI Log

- **Hide Repeats** folds a message SMAPI logs again and again into one line, with how many times.
- Earlier SMAPI logs are in the **Log File** menu, next to the latest and crash logs, with **Open Logs Folder**.
- **Copy Shown Messages** (⇧⌘C) copies just what the filters show; select lines to copy only those, or **Export Log…** to save the file.
- With the Loaded Mods panel open, SMAPI's and the game's versions show as icons (named on hover) instead of being cut off.
